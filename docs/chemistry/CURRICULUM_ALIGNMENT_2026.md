# 2026 curriculum comparison

Checked 2026-10-24. **The five supplied textbooks are not established as an
identical 2026 school curriculum.** This comparison does not change the selected
268-entry book map and supplies no scientific facts to lessons.

## Official publication trail

[UZBMB Qabul-2026](https://uzbmb.uz/page/qabul2026_2027) links to
[2026 programmes](https://uzbmb.uz/page/o_dastur2026), which separates
[grade9 admission](https://uzbmb.uz/page/o_dasturlar9) and
[grade11 admission](https://uzbmb.uz/page/o_dasturlar11). Their Chemistry pages
are [grade9 stream](https://uzbmb.uz/page/kimyo_dastur9) and
[grade11 stream](https://uzbmb.uz/page/kimyo_dastur11).
The generic `/page/kimyo_dastur` still links to qabul2025 files; it must not be
treated as the verified 2026 trail.

The official portal describes these as programmes for admission test formation,
provided by education authorities. Portal publication under `qabul2026` does not
mean a PDF was authored in 2026 or independently certify the current timetable of
every school. A newer ministry order and detailed outcome crosswalk remain
separate evidence requirements.

comparison; the decisive2022 grade7 pages15–18, grade10 pages17–18, covers and
representative older chapter pages were also visually reviewed. This is not a
claim of complete scientific/outcome review of every programme line.

# 2026–2027 alignment audit — incomplete

Checked 2026-10-26. The supplied subject-knowledge sources remain the six pinned
2017/2018/2019 textbooks. External sources are used only for programme alignment.

## What is actually evidenced

The [ministry's official FAQ](https://gov.uz/qr/olduzedu/faq) identifies the
2026-04-10 order No. 133 approving the 2026–2027 basic teaching plans. This
establishes the existence of that plan; it does not provide a complete history
learning-outcome mapping against these six textbook editions.

The [official regional teaching-plan page](https://qr.uzedu.uz/uz/services/6)
opened, but exposed an image rather than a dated, machine-readable full subject
programme in this inspection. The
[assessment agency's history programme index](https://uzbmb.uz/page/tarix_dastur)
was discoverable in search, but direct opening twice returned HTTP 502. It must
not be represented as a reviewed 2026–2027 programme PDF.

Secondary calendar-plan/reposted-PDF sites were found as discovery leads only.
Their labels are insufficient to certify the entire official subject programme.
No lesson fact or additional topic has been imported from them.
